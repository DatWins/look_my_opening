Each tree shows every line of an opening study on one page. Every position is a board, every branch is labeled with its move, and a green arrow shows the move just played. Click a tree to open it, then scroll to zoom in and drag to move around.

## Philidor Defence
1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 d6 — 20 lines branching from 3.d4: 3...exd4, 3...Nc6, 3...Nd7, 3...Bg4 and 3...Nf6.
